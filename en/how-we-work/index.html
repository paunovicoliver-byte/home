How we work

Every project starts with a conversation.

You don't need a finished drawing. A photograph, a sketch or a few measurements are enough to begin. From there, we take five steps together.

  1. 01

    Your idea

    Send us whatever you have. It does not need to be complete.

    • A photograph
    • A sketch
    • Measurements
    • Architectural plans
    • An inspiration image
    • A reference project
    • A written description
  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk it through — what you need, what the building or room asks for, what is possible.

    • Online
    • By telephone
    • By video call
    • In person through our Swiss contact presence
  3. 03

    Design & quotation

    We put everything on paper and agree on it together before any wood is cut.

    • Dimensions
    • Wood species
    • Finish
    • Construction
    • Details
    • Delivery location
    • Timeline
    • Price
  4. 04

    Handmade in Serbia

    Your piece is made by hand in our workshop. Typically this takes about 1–2 weeks after the final specifications are confirmed. Complex projects can take longer.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Shipping typically takes about one additional week, depending on destination and project size.

Made in Serbia. Delivered to Switzerland and across Europe.
