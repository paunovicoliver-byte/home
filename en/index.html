The atelier

Wood is not just a material. It is our philosophy.

DRVENO is a small family workshop. We make windows, doors, tables and pieces for homes and historic buildings — one at a time, by hand, from wood we know well.

Behind it are twenty-five years of working with wood and traditional crafts, many of them in Switzerland, and a return to Serbia to build the workshop that had long been a plan.

Read our story

Our speciality

Windows should breathe too.

Wooden windows are at the heart of our work. We have chosen wood over plastic for decades — because wood can be repaired, because it ages with dignity, and because a house deserves windows that belong to it.

Each window is built for one opening: its dimensions, its profiles, its architecture.

Heritage & restoration

Preserving what deserves to remain.

Old wooden windows should not automatically be replaced. We first try to understand how they were made — and then decide, together with you, whether to repair, reproduce or replace.

What we believe

Wood can breathe.

We do not want to cover nature with plastic and imitate what already exists naturally. We work with honest, natural materials.

  • No two pieces are alike

    Every tree grows differently. So does everything we make from it. Grain, knots and colour are characteristics, not defects.

  • Perfection cannot be rushed

    We do not hurry a piece to ship it sooner. It leaves the workshop when the craftsman is satisfied that it is right.

  • Made to stay

    Made slowly, so it can stay with you for a very long time — and be repaired, rather than replaced, when the years show.

From our forest

Closer to the material.

Our family has its own forest in Serbia, and part of the wood we work with can come from there.

It is not about a label. It is about knowing the material before it ever reaches the workbench — how a tree grew, where it stood, how it was dried.

How it works

From an idea to your home.

  1. 01

    Your idea

    Send us whatever you have. It does not need to be complete.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk it through — what you need, what the building or room asks for, what is possible.

  3. 03

    Design & quotation

    We put everything on paper and agree on it together before any wood is cut.

  4. 04

    Handmade in Serbia

    Your piece is made by hand in our workshop. Typically this takes about 1–2 weeks after the final specifications are confirmed. Complex projects can take longer.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Shipping typically takes about one additional week, depending on destination and project size.
