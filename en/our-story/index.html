Our Story

A workshop built after 25 years of learning.

DRVENO did not begin with a business plan. It began with a craftsman, a long apprenticeship to his material, and a decision to go home.

  1. Home
  2. Our Story
01 Roots

Where it began.

Our founder grew up in Serbia, among houses where wood was simply what things were made of — windows, doors, tables, roofs.

For him, wood has never been just a construction material. Wood is alive. It ages, it moves, it breathes, and it carries the marks of time.

02 Switzerland

Years of learning.

He spent many years living and working in Switzerland, gaining experience across traditional trades — woodworking and stonemasonry among them.

Switzerland taught him precision, patience and respect for things that are built to last. Those years still shape the way the workshop works today.

03 Conviction

Wood, not plastic.

Over decades he deliberately chose wood over materials such as plastic, particularly for windows.

He believes the objects around a home should be natural, repairable and able to become more beautiful with age.

04 Return

Coming home.

In his fifties, he decided to return to Serbia, where his roots are, and to build the workshop he had always dreamed of.

DRVENO is the result of that decision: a workshop focused entirely on craftsmanship, surrounded by nature and close to its raw material.

Every tree grows differently. So does everything we make from it.

From our forest

Closer to the material.

The family also has its own forest in Serbia. Part of the wood we work with can come from there.

That creates an unusually close relationship between craftsman and material — from tree to finished object. It is not simply about sustainability. It is about understanding and respecting the material before it ever reaches the workbench.

Today

A small workshop, by choice.

We are a family workshop and we intend to stay one. That allows us to make one piece at a time, to know every client, and to say honestly when something is — or is not — a good idea.

We work for homes in Serbia, Switzerland and across Europe, and for buildings that deserve to keep their character.
