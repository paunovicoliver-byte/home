Restoration · House

An old house, kept alive

Windows and an entrance door for a traditional family house — repaired where possible, rebuilt where necessary.

Project type
Restoration · House
Materials
  • Oak and larch
  • Oil and paint finishes

The project

The owners wanted to live in the house, not in a museum. But they also did not want to lose what made it theirs.

So we kept the original windows wherever the wood allowed, rebuilt the rest in the same language, and made a new entrance door to match.

The challenge

Windows of different ages and conditions, and a wish for more comfort without changing the face of the house.

Our approach

Repair first, reproduction second — every decision explained and agreed with the family.
