About this piece

A door is touched more often than almost anything else in a house. It should close with weight and quiet, and it should age with dignity.

We build panelled, ledged or plain doors, and we reproduce historic doors where the original can no longer be repaired.

Every tree grows differently. Grain, knots and colour vary from piece to piece — they are part of its character, not flaws.

Production & Delivery
