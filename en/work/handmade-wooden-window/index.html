About this piece

Our wooden windows are made one at a time, for one specific opening. We start with the building — its proportions, its walls, the light it receives — and only then decide on profiles, divisions and wood.

Solid timber can be sanded, re-oiled, re-painted and repaired, part by part, for decades. That is the reason we have chosen wood over plastic for most of our working lives.

Every tree grows differently. Grain, knots and colour vary from piece to piece — they are part of its character, not flaws.

Care
  • Wipe frames with a soft, damp cloth; avoid harsh cleaning agents.
  • Check the finish once a year, especially on the weather side and the bottom rail.
  • Refresh oil or paint as soon as the surface looks dry or worn — small, regular care is easier than large repairs.
